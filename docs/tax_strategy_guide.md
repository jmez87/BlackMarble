# Tax Strategy & Deductions Guide

## Overview
This comprehensive tax strategy system helps BLACK MARBLE Advisory Group clients maximize their tax savings through strategic deduction tracking and year-end planning.

## Key Features

### 📊 Dashboard Components
1. **Real-time Deduction Tracking**: Monitor tax-deductible expenses as they occur
2. **Automatic Calculations**: Built-in IRS rules and limits for 2024 tax year
3. **Tax Savings Estimates**: Calculate potential savings based on your tax bracket
4. **Visual Analytics**: Charts and graphs to identify trends and opportunities

### 💰 Supported Deduction Categories

#### Business Expenses
- **Office Supplies**: Computers, software, office equipment
- **Professional Development**: Training, conferences, certifications (up to $5,250)
- **Business Meals**: 50% deductible for business-related meals
- **Travel Expenses**: Airfare, hotels, car rentals for business trips
- **Home Office**: Simplified method ($5/sq ft, up to 300 sq ft = $1,500 max)
- **Professional Fees**: Legal, accounting, consulting services
- **Marketing & Advertising**: Website, promotional materials, advertising costs
- **Insurance**: Professional liability, business insurance premiums
- **Software & Technology**: Business software subscriptions, hardware
- **Communications**: Business phone, internet, mobile plans

#### Vehicle Expenses
- **Standard Mileage**: $0.67 per mile for business use (2024 rate)
- **Actual Expenses**: Gas, maintenance, insurance (business percentage only)

#### Retirement Contributions
- **SEP-IRA**: Up to 25% of compensation or $69,000 (2024)
- **Solo 401(k)**: Combined employee/employer contributions up to $69,000
- **Traditional IRA**: $7,000 limit ($8,000 if 50+)

#### Health & Benefits
- **Health Insurance**: Self-employed health insurance premiums
- **HSA Contributions**: $4,300 individual, $8,550 family (2024)
- **Dependent Care**: Up to $5,000 for dependent care expenses

## Usage Instructions

### Getting Started
1. **Install Dependencies**:
   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   ```

2. **Run the Dashboard**:
   ```bash
   streamlit run scripts/tax_strategy_dashboard.py
   ```

3. **Configure Settings**:
   - Set your marginal tax bracket in the sidebar
   - Select the appropriate tax year
   - Review deduction categories and limits

### Adding Expenses
1. Navigate to the "➕ Add Expense" tab
2. Enter the expense date, category, amount, and description
3. The system automatically calculates the deductible amount and tax savings
4. Special handling for:
   - Business meals (50% deduction)
   - Mileage (standard rate calculation)
   - Home office (simplified method)

### Monitoring Progress
- **Overview Tab**: See total deductions, tax savings, and category breakdowns
- **Analytics Tab**: Review quarterly summaries and efficiency metrics
- **Transaction Log**: Filter and search all recorded expenses

## Tax Strategy Recommendations

### Year-End Planning (Q4 Focus)
- **Accelerate Deductions**: Purchase needed equipment before December 31
- **Retirement Contributions**: Maximize SEP-IRA or Solo 401(k) contributions
- **Professional Development**: Register for conferences or training programs
- **Equipment Purchases**: Consider Section 179 deduction for large purchases

### Quarterly Actions
1. **Q1**: Set up tracking systems, establish baseline
2. **Q2**: Review mid-year progress, adjust estimated tax payments
3. **Q3**: Implement strategies, plan year-end purchases
4. **Q4**: Execute final deductions, prepare for tax season

### Documentation Best Practices
- **Keep Digital Receipts**: Scan or photograph all business receipts
- **Maintain Mileage Log**: Track business miles with dates and purposes
- **Separate Accounts**: Use dedicated business credit cards and bank accounts
- **Detailed Descriptions**: Record the business purpose for each expense

## Compliance Notes

### Record Keeping Requirements
- **7-Year Rule**: Keep tax records for at least 7 years
- **Digital Storage**: Cloud backup of all receipts and documentation
- **Business Purpose**: Document business justification for all expenses

### IRS Guidelines
- **Ordinary and Necessary**: Expenses must be ordinary and necessary for business
- **Business vs Personal**: Clear separation of business and personal expenses
- **Substantiation**: Adequate records to support all deductions
